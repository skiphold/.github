# SkipHold™ — The White-Label AI Communications Platform for Service Providers

SkipHold™ is a partner-driven, white-label AI communications platform and framework that MSPs, UCaaS providers, ITSPs and telecom partners deliver end-to-end to their customers under their own brand. AI and human teams work every conversation together: voice, SMS and WhatsApp.

🌐 https://skiphold.com · 🔐 https://admin.skiphold.com · 📅 https://skiphold.com/contact · 🤝 https://skiphold.com/partners · 📩 info@skiphold.com · 💬 https://community.skiphold.com

## What SkipHold does
- ✅ AI receptionist and AI voice agents (Receptionist, department Manager, personal Assistant), built with Prompt Tools™
- ✅ Conversations: one omni-channel inbox for voice, SMS and WhatsApp, with human takeover
- ✅ Connections: built-in CRM and help desk
- ✅ Native PBX with branded desktop and mobile softphones and a built-in web phone
- ✅ AI warm transfers and live call monitoring
- ✅ Meeting Join™: AI agents join Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls
- ✅ White-label Stripe billing, multi-tenant portal and A2P 10DLC compliance support

Native or bring-your-own at every layer. Your stack, our framework. SkipHold deploys, trains and supports the offering, so you don't have to build it.

## Connects to your network
- Native: Gemelos PBX
- SIP interop: NetSapiens, Thirdlane, 3CX, FreePBX, FusionPBX and other standards-based SIP platforms
- Carriers: Twilio, Telnyx, Bandwidth and other carriers

## Built for providers
MSPs / MSSPs · UCaaS providers · ITSPs / VoIP providers · Hosted PBX operators · Telecom resellers & master agents
